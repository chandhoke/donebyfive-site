How it works

Open the Weekly Data tab, type in the week's sales, and switch to the dashboard. There are no macros to enable and no add-ins to install — it's a clean spreadsheet that opens anywhere. Why track by category

Total sales tell you whether it was a good week. Category sales tell you why. When you can see that beverages carried the week while frozen quietly flatlined, you order smarter, set your shelf space with intent, and catch a fading department before it ties up cash. That's the difference between reacting at month-end and steering week to week.
