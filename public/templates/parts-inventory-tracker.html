A parts tracker for repair shops that shows what you have, what is running low, and what to reorder.

Who it's for

Phone, computer, and electronics repair shops that keep parts on the shelf and lose track of them. If you have ever started a repair and found the part was not there, this is built for you.

What's inside

  • Parts Inventory — One row per part: what it is, how many you have, and what it costs.
  • Low Stock — Parts that have dropped to low stock, flagged so they do not get missed.
  • Reorder Log — A log of what you reordered and when, so the same part does not get ordered twice.

How it works

List your parts and current quantities, then update the count as parts go into repairs. Low-stock parts surface on their own sheet, and the reorder log keeps your ordering history in one place. No macros, no add-ins.
