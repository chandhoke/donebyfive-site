Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

DoneByFive ("we", "us") sells retail operations spreadsheet templates and is based in Alberta, Canada. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we handle it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Your email address — only if you choose to enter it in our "Notify me" signup form. We never collect it any other way.
  • Privacy-friendly analytics — we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general traffic (page views, referrers). It is cookieless, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not collect personal information.

How we use your email

If you sign up, we use your email only to notify you about new templates and product updates — roughly one email per launch. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for their own marketing.

Where your information is stored

Signup emails are stored using Cloudflare (Cloudflare D1), which also hosts this website. Your data is handled subject to Cloudflare's security practices.

Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our analytics provider is cookieless, so no cookie banner is required for normal use of this site.

Your choices & CASL

You can ask to be removed from our list at any time by emailing us (below), and every email we send will include an unsubscribe option. We aim to honour removal requests promptly. This is consistent with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL): we only email people who have asked to hear from us, we identify ourselves, and we make it easy to opt out.

Data retention

We keep your email address until you ask us to remove it or unsubscribe.

Children

This site is intended for business owners and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available here, with the "Last updated" date at the top.

Contact

Questions, or want to be removed from our list? Email hello@donebyfive.com.
