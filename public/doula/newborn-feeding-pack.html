FAQ

Does it cover bottle feeding too, or only breastfeeding?

Both. The pack covers breastfeeding/chestfeeding, pumping and storage, and bottle/combo feeding — it's built to support any feeding choice without judgement.

Is it really bilingual (English and French)?

Yes. Every guide is provided in both English and French, so you can use whichever you prefer.

Is this medical advice?

No. These are general-education guides for informational purposes, within a doula's scope of practice. For latch concerns, low supply, or mastitis, the guides point you to a lactation consultant or your provider — they don't replace clinical care.
