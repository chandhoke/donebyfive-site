Built the DoneByFive way — bilingual and plain

The same standard runs through the doula line as the retail templates: bilingual English and French in one file, plain-language writing you can read tired, instant download, and buy-once pricing with no subscription. The difference is the audience. These toolkits are written to respect a doula's scope of practice — they're general education and non-medical support, and every medical-adjacent guide says so plainly and points you to your provider when a question belongs there.

Who are they for? Doulas and birth workers building a consistent client library, and parents who'd rather have one calm, trustworthy resource than a hundred browser tabs. Start with a featured bundle above, or browse the full catalogue — including the specialization kits — on Etsy.
