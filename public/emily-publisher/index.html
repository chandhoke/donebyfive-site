Emily Publisher

Emily Publisher is a single user personal application used by Emily Live to publish videos to her own YouTube channel, Emily Live (@itsemilylive).

The application is not offered to the public and does not provide accounts for other users. It is used only by the channel owner to upload her own videos, set the title, description and publishing options for those videos, apply YouTube's synthetic media disclosure where required, and post the owner's first comment on a newly published video.
