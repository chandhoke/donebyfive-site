Emily Publisher Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Emily Publisher is a single user personal application operated by Emily Live. It is used only to publish videos to the operator's own YouTube channel, Emily Live (@itsemilylive). It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from visitors to this page.

Google user data accessed

When the operator authorizes Emily Publisher with Google, the application receives OAuth access and refresh tokens for the operator's Google account. With the YouTube permissions granted by the operator, the application can access the identifiers needed to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel, process the video files and metadata the operator chooses to publish, and create a first comment on the operator's own newly published videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to authenticate the operator, upload and publish the operator's own videos, set publishing metadata and disclosures for those videos, post the operator's first comment, and verify that an upload was published correctly. Emily Publisher does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or any purpose unrelated to publishing the operator's own videos.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored in a private local token store on the operator's computer and are not published on this website. Video files and publishing metadata are processed only as needed to complete an upload requested by the operator.

Sharing

Emily Publisher does not sell Google user data and does not share it with third parties for their own use. Data is sent to Google and YouTube APIs only as necessary to carry out the publishing actions described above.

Limited Use disclosure

Emily Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are retained until access is revoked or the token is deleted by the operator. The operator can revoke Emily Publisher's access at any time through the third party access controls in their Google Account. Requests about this policy or deletion of locally stored tokens can be sent to its.emily.live@gmail.com.
